Our Infrastructure

Quality roller skating rinks and dedicated training tracks powering athletes across Coimbatore district.

World-Class Training Grounds in Kovai

Coimbatore boasts some of the finest public roller sports infrastructure in Tamil Nadu. Managed in collaboration with municipal bodies and sports institutions, our rinks provide safe, high-speed surfaces where grassroots beginners grow into state champions and national medalists.

Premier Competitive Hub

VOC Skating Rink

VOC Park Grounds, Gopalapuram, Coimbatore

The VOC Park Skating Rink is Coimbatore's flagship roller skating stadium, situated centrally in the iconic VOC Park complex near Nehru Stadium and Gandhipuram. Built with professional banked turns and a specialized smooth finish, it is the primary battleground for district meets and selection trials.

Grassroots & Foundation Rink

Gandhi Park Skating Rink

Gandhi Park, Selvapuram / R.S. Puram, Coimbatore

Located in the lush greenery of historic Gandhi Park serving western Coimbatore, this rink is the premier grass-roots nursery for young skaters. Equipped with a level flat track and protective fencing, it provides a safe, welcoming environment for toddlers and beginners building their balance.

Facility Guidelines & Skater Safety

To maintain athlete safety and surface longevity across all CDSA-affiliated rinks, skaters and parents are requested to adhere to standard rink protocols:

Mandatory Safety Gear

Helmets, knee guards, elbow guards, and wrist guards are required at all times on the rink during training sessions.

Wheel Care & Surface Protection

Skaters must ensure wheels and stoppers are clean of debris, rocks, and mud before entering the skating surface.

Coaching Enquiries

For training registrations, batch timings, and coaching academy admissions, visit either rink during evening practice hours (5:30 PM – 7:30 PM).
