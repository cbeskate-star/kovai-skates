Governing Association Overview

About CDSA

The Coimbatore District Skating Association (CDSA) has governed, developed, and promoted roller sports disciplines across Coimbatore district for over 36 years.

Institutional Profile

Who We Are

CDSA unites the registered skating clubs, coaches, schools, and sporting families across Coimbatore district. We organize the annual district championships, enforce athletic safety and competition standards, and prepare our skaters to qualify and excel at state, national, and world events.

Our official emblem proudly reflects the major competitive disciplines governed by the association: Speed Skating, Artistic Skating, and Roller Hockey.

  • Speed Skating (Quad & Inline)
  • Artistic Skating
  • Roller Hockey
  • Alpine & Downhill
  • Inline Freestyle
  • Skateboarding

Institutional Governance

Affiliation Hierarchy

CDSA is affiliated to the Tamilnadu Roller Skating Association (TNRSA), which is affiliated to the Roller Skating Federation of India (India Skate), recognized by the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu (SDAT) and MYAS, and recognized internationally by World Skate under the IOC.

  1. District Governing Body
    District

    Coimbatore District Skating Association

    Conducts district championships and supports clubs, coaches, and skaters across Coimbatore. Regd. No. 302/2014.

    Regd. 302/2014 Host Body
  3. State Governing Body
    State

    Tamilnadu Roller Skating Association

    Conducts the Tamil Nadu State Roller Skating Championship. Recognized state sports governing body.

    Reg. No. 63/1992 SDAT L-18-31523
  5. National Federation
    National

    Roller Skating Federation of India

    The national governing body for roller sports (India Skate), recognized by the Government of India.

    India Skate MYAS Recognized
  7. World Federation
    International

    World Skate

    The international governing body for all roller sports disciplines, officially recognized by the IOC.

    Lausanne, Switzerland IOC Recognized
