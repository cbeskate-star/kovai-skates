About CDSA

The Coimbatore District Skating Association promotes and organises roller sports across Coimbatore district.

Who we are

CDSA brings together the skating clubs, coaches, schools and families of Coimbatore district. We conduct the district roller skating championships and help our skaters move on to state and national events. In 2026, CDSA is the host association for the 36th Tamil Nadu State Roller Skating Championship.

Our emblem shows three of the sports we serve: roller hockey, artistic skating and speed skating.

  • Speed skating (quad and inline)
  • Artistic skating
  • Roller hockey
  • Alpine and downhill

Affiliation

CDSA is affiliated to the Tamilnadu Roller Skating Association (TNRSA), the state body for roller sports. TNRSA is affiliated to the Roller Skating Federation of India (India Skate) and recognised by the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu.

  1. District

    Coimbatore District Skating Association

    Conducts district championships and supports clubs and skaters in Coimbatore. Regd. No. 302/2014.

  3. State

    Tamilnadu Roller Skating Association

    Conducts the Tamil Nadu State Roller Skating Championship. Reg. No. 63/1992. SDAT Recognition No. L-18-31523.

    Dr. R. Pratap Kumar, PresidentTNCM. Chockalingam, General SecretaryS. Rajusundaram, Treasurer

  5. National

    Roller Skating Federation of India

    The national body for roller sports (India Skate), recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports and affiliated to World Skate.
