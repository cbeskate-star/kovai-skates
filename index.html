Skating Association

With over 36 years of experience and excellence, the Coimbatore District Skating Association (CDSA) has nurtured and produced numerous national and international skaters. As the governing body for roller sports in Coimbatore, we conduct district championships and partner with clubs, coaches, and schools to guide athletes from their first rink strides to global podiums.

Skating Associations: District to International

From grassroots coaching in Coimbatore to the International Olympic Committee, CDSA operates within the recognized pyramid of state, national, and world roller sports governance.

  1. District Governing Body
    District

    Coimbatore District Skating Association

    Conducts district championships and supports clubs, coaches, schools and skaters in Coimbatore district.

    Regd. No. 302/2014 Host Body
  3. State Governing Body
    State

    Tamilnadu Roller Skating Association

    Conducts the Tamil Nadu State Roller Skating Championship. Recognized state sports governing body.

    Reg. No. 63/1992 SDAT L-18-31523
  5. National Federation
    National

    Roller Skating Federation of India

    The national governing body for roller sports (India Skate), recognized by the Government of India.

    India Skate MYAS Recognized
  7. World Federation
    International

    World Skate

    The international governing body for all roller sports disciplines, officially recognized by the IOC.

    Lausanne, Switzerland IOC Recognized
