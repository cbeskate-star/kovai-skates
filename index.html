CDSA is the district body for roller sports in Coimbatore. We conduct the district championships and work with clubs, coaches and schools so that young skaters can grow from their first rink session to state and national competition.

Skating Associations: District to International

  1. District

    Coimbatore District Skating Association

    Conducts district championships and supports clubs and skaters in Coimbatore. Regd. No. 302/2014.

  3. State

    Tamilnadu Roller Skating Association

    Conducts the Tamil Nadu State Roller Skating Championship. Reg. No. 63/1992. SDAT Recognition No. L-18-31523.

  5. National

    Roller Skating Federation of India

    The national governing body for roller sports (India Skate), recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.

  7. International

    World Skate

    The international governing body for all roller sports disciplines, officially recognised by the International Olympic Committee (IOC).
