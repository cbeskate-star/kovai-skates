Infrastructure Matrix

Championship Venues Developed & Utilized

The physical facilities built, renovated, and commissioned across Coimbatore and Pollachi as documented in the association minute records.

Municipal Flagship Hub

V.O.C. Park Skating Ground

The beating heart of Coimbatore skating. Allotted by Coimbatore Corporation in 2016, transformed with ₹40L synthetic banked surface in 2017, and completely overhauled under Namakku Naame scheme with ₹25L matching investment in 2023.

Surface: Synthetic Banked Track Disciplines: Speed, Roller Hockey Capacity: 1,500 Spectators Location: V.O.C. Park, Coimbatore
International 200m Track

Pollachi International Rink

200-meter international-standard banked speed track. Hosted the 2022 State Speed Championship and the Speed Event of India Skate Games 2024 with full RSFI officiating.

Surface: 200m Parabolic Banked Rink Disciplines: Speed (Quad & Inline) Capacity: 2,000 Spectators Location: Pollachi, Coimbatore Dist.
Dedicated Hockey Arena

R.S. Puram Gandhi Park

Constructed in 2024 as part of a ₹50L dual-hockey rink initiative with Coimbatore Corporation and J.S. Association to provide a dedicated permanent home for district Roller and Inline Hockey.

Surface: Specialized Hockey Flooring Disciplines: Roller & Inline Hockey Enclosure: High-Impact Perimeter Boards Location: R.S. Puram, Coimbatore
High-Velocity Road Circuit

Kari Motor Speedway

Commissioned for the road racing legs of the 2017 Tamil Nadu State Selection Meet, providing an expansive, professional racing tarmac free of vehicular traffic.

Surface: FIA-Grade Asphalt Circuit Disciplines: Road Long Distance & Points Safety: Controlled Circuit Runoffs Location: Chettipalayam, Coimbatore
Alpine & Downhill Descent

Nallampalli Bridge Course

Official sanctioned venue for the downhill slalom and inline alpine trials of India Skate Games 2024 and the 2025 National Selection Meets, where World Skate qualifiers were scouted.

Surface: Engineered Gradient Tarmac Disciplines: Alpine & Downhill Slalom Elevation: Steep Descent Course Location: Pollachi Rural Corridor
Artistic Championship Hall

Keshar Vidhya Mandir Hall

High-ceiling international standard indoor arena hosted the Artistic events of India Skate Games 2024 and the National Selection Championship with official video releases by Maharashtra Governor.

Surface: Polished Maple/Hardwood Floor Disciplines: Artistic, Figures, Solo Dance Facilities: High-Lumen Arena Lighting Location: Pollachi Sector

Leadership Election & International Selection

The meeting observed silent tribute to late Founder President Mr. A. Sikander Basha (who passed away on 08.09.2016), resolved to unveil his portrait at district selection meets, and instituted the annual Individual Championship trophy in his memory.

Subject Official Resolution Adopted
Executive Election Senior Vice President Mr. A. Sudhakaran unanimously elected as new President. Mr. V. S. Chandrasekar confirmed as Secretary, Mr. T. S. R. Arunprasad as Vice President, Mr. S. V. Ramanathan as Treasurer. New members Mr. R. Kathirvelu and Mr. P. Mohanasundar co-opted.
World Championship Special appreciation and formal best wishes conveyed to district skater Mr. P. Rahul Pandian on his qualification to represent India at the World Speed Skating Championship in Nanjing, China.
Digital Platform Resolved to launch and operate the association's official portal coimbatoreskating.com for digital announcements and transparent circulars across the district.
Banking & Audit Authorized President, Secretary, and Treasurer to operate the SBI C.T.O. Branch account, audit the 2015–2016 accounts, and renew the society registration.

Coimbatore Corporation V.O.C. Ground Allotment

Historic resolution formally recording the Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation council order granting the V.O.C. Park Skating Ground to CDSA for 3 years maintenance.

Corporation Gratitude Formally conveyed thanks to Honorable Mayor Dr. T. Ganapathi Rajkumar and Commissioner Mr. Vijay Karthikeyan IAS for unanimously passing the council resolution entrusting V.O.C. Skating Ground to CDSA for a 3-year term.
Synthetic Rink Plan Resolved to officially petition the Mayor and Commissioner to level and resurface the broken concrete rink into an international-grade synthetic banked ground to eliminate children's injuries.

Chapter 5 • 2024

India Skate Games & World Skate Games Italy Qualification

Meeting 13 (AGM) • Source: Book pp. 6–11 • 23 Resolutions
Meeting 13 (AGM) 01 September 2024
PDF pp. 26–31 • Book pp. 6–11 • Sunday 3:30 PM

Hosting India Skate Games & Sending Athletes to World Championships

The pinnacle sporting chapter: RSFI awarded hosting rights for India Skate Games 2024 across 11 disciplines, leading to 2 new roller hockey arenas and qualification for the 2024 World Skate Games in Italy.

Subject Official Resolution Adopted
India Skate Games Host Formally recorded RSFI and TNRSA's award to host the 11-discipline India Skate Games 2024. Coimbatore Member of Parliament Dr. P. Ganapathy Rajkumar appointed Event Chairman.
2 New Hockey Rinks Constructed two brand-new Roller Hockey Rinks at R. S. Puram Gandhi Park and V.O.C. Ground (estimated ₹50,00,000 project) with Coimbatore Corporation and J.S. Association.
Italian Coach Clinic Conducted elite high-performance speed skating camp at V.O.C. Rink with Italian National Coach Mr. Giulio Ravasi for 26 district skaters.
World Skate Games (Italy) Formally felicitated Coimbatore athletes Kishore Karuna and Ishwarya on their qualification in Downhill to represent Team India at the 2024 World Skate Games in Italy.
National Honors Congratulated Mr. R. Prakash on earning National Referee Certification in Delhi, and TNRSA President Dr. R. Prathap Kumar on his appointment as Indian Team Manager for World Skate Games Italy.
Multi-Venue Games Conducted Speed at Pollachi International Rink, Alpine at Nallampalli Bridge, Artistic at Keshar Vidhya Mandir, Hockey at Gandhi Park and V.O.C., Downhill at Karamadai. Graced by Ministers, MPs, Corporation Commissioner Sivaguru Prabhakaran IAS, and RSFI President Thulsiram Agarwal.
